I · Cohort mathhow we got to 144

We started with 578 YC AI startups. We focused on the post-ChatGPT founder generation — companies founded in 2023 or later (534 remain). We dropped companies marked dead, pivoted, or dormant (–63). Finally, we dropped companies showing zero positive momentum signals over the last 180 days (–327).

A positive momentum signal is one of five: recent news, a fresh website (≤90 days), open job listings, recent blog posts, or active GitHub commits. We require at least one. 144 companies clear the bar.
