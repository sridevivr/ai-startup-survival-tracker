How we got to 144

We started with 578 YC AI startups. We narrowed to companies founded in 2023 or later (534) to focus on the post-ChatGPT founder generation. We cut companies marked dead, pivoted, or dormant (–63). We then cut companies showing zero positive momentum signals over the last 180 days (–327). That leaves 144 companies with at least one sign of life: recent news, a fresh website, active hiring, recent blog posts, or active GitHub commits.
